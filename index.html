Medical Imaging Courses at RPI

Two medical imaging courses in the Department of Biomedical Engineering, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute: the undergraduate Bio-Imaging and Bio-Instrumentation (BMED 2300), taught by Dr. Ge Wang and Dr. Hisham Mohamed, and Medical Imaging (BMED 4590/6590), taught by Dr. Ge Wang. Every lecture is also taught interactively on ALIVE.
